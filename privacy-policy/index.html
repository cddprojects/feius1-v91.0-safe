Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

When you submit the contact form on the Site, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, the topic you select, and the content of your message. Please do not include sensitive personal information, such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or passwords, in your message.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and the date and time of your visit. This information is typically collected in server logs.

Information collected through cookies and advertising technologies

We use Google Ads and related Google measurement tools to measure the performance of our advertising and to show our ads to people who may be interested in them. These tools use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to collect information about your visit and interactions with the Site, such as the ad you clicked, pages you viewed, actions you took (for example, submitting the contact form), and your IP address, device, and browser information. See Section 4 for details.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • operate, maintain, and secure the Site;
  • review and respond to your messages and requests;
  • understand which topics are useful to our readers and improve our content;
  • measure and improve the performance of our online advertising, including showing our ads to people who have previously visited the Site;
  • detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or technical issues; and
  • comply with applicable law and enforce our rights.

3. How We Disclose Information

We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money. We may disclose information:

  • To service providers that host the Site or help us operate it, who may use the information only to perform services for us.
  • To advertising and measurement partners, such as Google, through cookies and similar technologies, so we can measure and show our ads. Under some state privacy laws, this may be considered “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or “targeted advertising.” You can opt out as described in Sections 4 and 8.
  • For legal reasons, when we believe disclosure is required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Extra Hours, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our partners use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember your preferences, measure the performance of our advertising, and deliver ads.

Google advertising

We use Google Ads, including conversion tracking and remarketing. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to the Site and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to the Site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services (opens in a new tab), and read Google’s Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

Fonts

The Site loads web fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google receives certain technical information, such as your IP address.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings (opens in a new tab).
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at optout.aboutads.info (opens in a new tab) or optout.networkadvertising.org (opens in a new tab).
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. If you block cookies, some features of the Site may not work as expected.
  • We honor opt-out preference signals, such as Global Privacy Control, where required by applicable law.

Our Site does not currently respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals, because there is no common industry standard for them.

5. Third-Party Websites

The Site contains links to websites operated by government agencies and other third parties. We do not control and are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to resolve disputes, and to comply with our legal obligations. After that, we delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request that we:

  • tell you what personal information we have collected about you and how we use it;
  • provide a copy of your personal information;
  • correct inaccurate personal information; and
  • delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions; and
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising.

Residents of California and certain other U.S. states have these rights under state privacy laws. You also have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising them. You can opt out of advertising-related tracking using the choices in Section 4. To make any other request, please contact us using the information in Section 11. We may need to verify your identity before we process your request, and you may use an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date above. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to make a privacy request, please contact us through the contact form on our home page and include “Privacy Request” in your message.
