Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Extra Hours collects when you use this site and how we use it. We’ve kept it short and in plain language.

Information you give us

If you use the feedback form, we receive what you type into it: your name, your email address (optional), the topic you pick, and your message. Please don’t include sensitive details such as your Social Security number, bank information, or passwords.

How we use it

  • To read your feedback and decide which topics to cover next.
  • To reply to you, if you shared an email address and asked a question.
  • To keep this site working and improve it.

We don’t sell your personal information.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. We use this information to keep the site secure and running.

Fonts and third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google receives technical information such as your IP address. Please see Google’s Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab) for details.

Pages on this site link to government and other outside websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we aren’t responsible for them.

Cookies

This site doesn’t set its own cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we’ll update this page and explain your choices.

How long we keep information

We keep feedback messages only as long as we need them to review and reply. You can ask us to delete a message you sent.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you sent us. Residents of some states have additional rights under state privacy laws. Use the feedback form on our home page to make a request, and tell us what you’d like us to do.

Children

This site is meant for adults looking for work. It isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top.

This page is general information, not legal advice.
